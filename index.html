Welcome to Wildlife Rescue Center

Our Wildlife Rescue Center helps injured and orphaned animals. We provide care and rehabilitation until the animals are ready to return to their natural habitat.

Our team works every day to protect wildlife and educate the community about animal care and conservation.

Animals We Help

Raccoons

We care for young and injured raccoons until they are healthy enough to return to the wild.

Tortoises

Our team provides food and safe habitats for tortoises that need rehabilitation.
