About Us

Wild Rescues is a nonprofit organization that helps injured, orphaned, and sick wildlife.

Our goal is to rescue animals, provide the care they need, and release them back into their natural environment when possible.

We Help Many Types of Wildlife

  • Raccoons
  • Birds
  • Rabbits
  • Turtles
  • Squirrels
  • Other local wildlife

Our Mission

We want to protect wildlife and teach people how they can help animals in their community.
